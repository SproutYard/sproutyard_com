Mighty Timer support

Mighty Timer knows the time, temperature and amount for your favourite teas, and tells you when your cup is ready.

Get it on the App Store.

Common questions

My alert didn’t sound. Check that notifications are on for Mighty Timer in Settings > Notifications, and that a Focus isn’t silencing them. In the app, Settings > Alarm chooses the sound and how many alerts you get.

Where’s my brew on the Lock Screen? Start a brew, then lock your phone. If it doesn’t appear, check that Live Activities are on in the app’s Settings, and for Mighty Timer in Settings > Mighty Timer.

Will my teas come with me when I update? Yes. Your teas, favourites and settings move into Mighty Timer 2.0 the first time you open it.

Where did the Apple Watch app go? It retired with Mighty Timer 2.0. Teas saved only on a watch don’t carry over.

Mighty Timer privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Mighty Timer is made by Sprout Yard Mobile Inc. This policy covers the Mighty Timer app for iPhone and iPad.

Your teas stay on your device

Your teas, recipes and favourites are kept on your iPhone or iPad, and in your device’s backups. There’s no account, and we never see them.

Usage analytics

Mighty Timer sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, our analytics provider. For example:

  • that a brew started or finished, with the tea’s category (such as “green”) and a rough length (such as “1–3 minutes”)
  • which settings were chosen, such as Celsius or dark mode
  • rounded counts, such as “6–20 teas” in your library

Mighty Timer never sends tea names, notes, searches or anything else you type.

Contact

Questions? Email info@sproutyard.com. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply.

For help with the app, see Mighty Timer support.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and its date.
